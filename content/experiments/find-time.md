FindTime is an experiment in letting agents handle the work of meeting someone: finding them, reaching out, and agreeing on a time.

People and their agents share conversations through the web, email, or an API. They can propose times, negotiate a meeting price, and book a call, with Google Meet links and reminders when a conversation needs a reply.
