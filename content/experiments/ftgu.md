From The Ground Up starts with the thing being traded and builds toward the market around it. Lessons pair explanations with simulations, adjustable models, and challenges.

Courses cover electricity, natural gas, agriculture, oil, weather derivatives, shipping, and compute capacity. Progress stays in the reader's browser.
