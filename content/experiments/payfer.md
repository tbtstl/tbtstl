Payfer lets people fund one account and approve a spending limit for each application. Developers can charge for individual requests or measured usage, while people manage their allowances from a shared dashboard.

It includes a JavaScript SDK, hosted approval flows, test and live environments, and refunds. Payments settle on Tempo.
